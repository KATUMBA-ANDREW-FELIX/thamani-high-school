Project 2026-A · Science & Tech

Modern Science Lab Equipment Drive

75% Funded

Upgrading the school's Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories with modern digital microscopes, precision balances, spectrophotometers, and safety equipment for practical UNEB exams.

Funding Progress: UGX 33.7M / UGX 45M 75%
Project 2026-B · ICT Innovation

Digital Library & Coding Hub Expansion

50% Funded

Procuring 30 new high-spec desktop computers, setting up high-speed fibre internet routers, and providing interactive smartboards to enhance computer science & coding skills.

Funding Progress: UGX 30M / UGX 60M 50%
