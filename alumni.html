A Message to Our Alumni

Your story didn’t end at graduation

The Thamani High School Alumni Association brings together former students from every class year into one community, united by shared memories and a shared commitment to the school.

Membership is open to every former student. Whether you can give an afternoon to speak at Career Week, sponsor a bright student, or simply reconnect with your year group, there is a place for you here.

Membership Benefits

Why join the Association

Membership connects you to a lifelong network and a meaningful role in the school’s future.

01

Professional Network

Connect with alumni across engineering, medicine, law, finance, technology and public service, in Uganda and abroad.

02

Student Mentorship

Guide O-Level and A-Level students through career talks, university preparation and internship referrals.

03

School Development

Shape and support projects that upgrade laboratories, ICT facilities, scholarships and sports infrastructure.

04

Reunions & Events

Priority invitations to the Annual Alumni Dinner, OB vs OG Sports Gala, class reunions and Founders’ Day.

Achievements

What we have built together

Milestones made possible by the generosity and commitment of our alumni community.

2025

15 full scholarships awarded

The Needy Student Endowment now covers full tuition, uniforms and scholastic materials for fifteen students from Wakiso District.

2024

First Annual Career Week

Over 40 alumni professionals returned to campus to mentor more than 600 students on career paths and university choices.

2023

Library digitisation launched

Alumni contributions funded the first phase of the school’s Digital Library, giving every student access to e-resources.
